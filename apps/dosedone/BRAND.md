# DoseDone — Brand

Senior-friendly medication reminder — big-button UX. App #8 of the portfolio
(queue order; claimed as `seniormeds`).

**Name note:** "PillPal" and "MedMinder" are taken (pill dispenser startups);
Medisafe/MyTherapy own the dense-tracker lane. **DoseDone** is free — no app
collisions found. The name is the core loop: take the dose, mark it done.
Package: `com.dosedone.app`.

## Identity

**Care — clinical calm, human warmth.** Deep teal (trust, clinical) with a
warm amber "TAKE" action color. Maximum contrast, giant type, huge targets —
designed for 70-year-old thumbs and eyes first. Nothing like Palate's
saffron, DrySpell's dawn, or LanceLedger's pine.

## Voice

Patient, reassuring, plain words. Short sentences. No jargon, no shame about
missed doses — "tomorrow is a fresh start." Standing disclaimer: not medical
advice; the app reminds, it does not prescribe.

## Accessibility rules (this app's whole point)

- Base body text 18sp (not 16), display 36.
- Minimum touch target 56dp (not 48) on primary actions.
- Text contrast AAA where possible; never place body text on tinted surfaces.
- One action per screen section; no nested menus.

## Differentiation vs Medisafe (Play repetitive-content thresholds)

1. **Senior-first big-button UX** — giant type, huge targets, minimal screens.
   Medisafe is feature-dense; this is the opposite.
2. **Caregiver summary** — weekly adherence report screen designed to be shown
   to family ("Here's how Mom did this week").
3. **Refill countdown with low-stock alerts** built into the take flow.
4. **No account, local-first, AR/EN RTL bilingual.**
