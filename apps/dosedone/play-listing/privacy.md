# Privacy Policy — DoseDone

**Effective date:** [launch date] · **Contact:** [support email]

DoseDone ("the app") is a medication reminder. This policy explains what
data we handle and why.

## Data stored on your device

Your medication data — medicine names, dosages, schedules, dose logs, pill
counts — is stored locally on your device using the app's private storage.
It never leaves your device unless you enable cloud backup (see below).

## Optional cloud backup

If cloud backup is enabled, an encrypted-in-transit copy of your medicines
and dose logs is stored on our backend (Supabase) keyed by a random device
identifier. No name, email, or account is required or collected. You can
delete the backup by erasing the app's data or contacting us.

## Notifications

Dose reminders are scheduled locally on your device. Reminder content never
leaves your device.

## Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Google Play. We receive purchase tokens to
verify your subscription status; we do not see or store your payment
details. Google's privacy policy applies to the payment itself.

## What we do NOT collect

- No advertising identifiers, no ad SDKs, no behavioral tracking.
- No location data.
- No contacts, photos, or files.

## Health data

Medication data is personal health information you choose to record. It
stays on your device (or in your optional backup) and is never sold,
shared, or used for advertising.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

## Changes

We will update this policy with any material change and note the new
effective date above.
