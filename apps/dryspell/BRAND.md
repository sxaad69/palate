# DrySpell — Brand

Sobriety counter with money-saved math. App #4 of the portfolio (queue order).

**Name note:** originally "ClearDays" per the queue, but collision check found a live
product in this exact niche (cleardays.co — "Drink less. Make room for more." — plus
Android app `com.cleardays.test`). Renamed to **DrySpell**: "dry" = not drinking,
"spell" = a stretch of time. No collisions found in the sobriety niche.
Package: `com.dryspell.app`.

## Identity

**Dawn — new beginnings.** Soft sky blue warming into gentle gold. Calm, hopeful,
fresh-start. Nothing like Palate's saffron/stone (food) — this is morning light.

- Primary accent: sky blue (trust, calm, clarity)
- Highlight: gentle gold (savings, milestones, celebration — money glows warm)
- Neutrals: cool slate (morning coolness)
- Dark mode: deep night blue → surfaces lift lighter, sky lightens so it doesn't vibrate

## Voice

Warm, non-judgmental, honest. "Every clear day counts." No shame language, no
medical claims — always "not medical advice; talk to a professional" where health
is mentioned. Global brand; AR/EN RTL is a feature, not the identity.

## Differentiation vs Trifoil's Sober (Play repetitive-content thresholds)

1. **Savings goals** — allocate money saved toward tangible goals with progress
   bars ("Weekend trip: $340 / $1,200"), not just a running total.
2. **Craving SOS mode** — one-tap crisis screen: breathing timer, your personal
   "why" reasons, and a daily pledge. Built for the hardest 10 minutes.
3. **Health recovery timeline** — body-repair milestones with the science of what
   heals when (sleep, liver, blood pressure…), celebrated, never diagnosing.
4. Multi-habit in one app (alcohol, smoking, other) — Trifoil splits these into
   separate apps; one home for all of them.
