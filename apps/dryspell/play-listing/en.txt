DrySpell: Sobriety Counter

SHORT DESCRIPTION (max 80 chars)
Count clear days, track money saved, and ride out cravings.

FULL DESCRIPTION
Every clear day counts.

DrySpell is a calm, beautiful sobriety counter for anyone cutting back or
quitting alcohol — and it works for smoking and other habits too.

COUNT YOUR CLEAR DAYS
A live counter shows your days, hours, minutes and seconds of clarity. Watch
the number grow — it's the most motivating number you'll own.

SEE YOUR MONEY COME BACK
Enter what you used to spend per day and DrySpell shows your running savings
total. Set savings goals — a weekend trip, a new laptop — and watch your
clear days fund them.

RIDE OUT CRAVINGS (SOS MODE)
When a craving hits, one tap opens SOS: a guided breathing pacer, your own
personal reasons for quitting, and your daily pledge. Cravings peak and pass —
usually within 10–20 minutes. This screen gets you through them.

CELEBRATE RECOVERY MILESTONES
A timeline of body-recovery milestones — 24 hours, 1 week, 1 month, 1 year —
each celebrated when you reach it. See how far you've come.

PRIVATE JOURNAL & ACHIEVEMENTS
Write a line a day, unlock achievement badges for streaks and savings, and
take a daily pledge to lock in your intention.

DrySpell is local-first: your data lives on your device, with optional cloud
backup. No account needed. No shame, no judgment — just a fresh start, one
day at a time.

DrySpell is a self-tracking tool, not medical care. If stopping feels unsafe,
please talk to a qualified professional first.
