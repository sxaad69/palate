# Privacy Policy — DrySpell

**Effective date:** [launch date] · **Contact:** [support email]

DrySpell ("the app") is a sobriety counter and self-tracking tool. This
policy explains what data we handle and why.

## Data stored on your device

Your sobriety data — start date, habit, daily spend, journal entries,
savings goals, personal reasons, pledge history — is stored locally on your
device using the app's private storage. It never leaves your device unless
you enable cloud backup (see below).

## Optional cloud backup

If cloud backup is enabled, an encrypted-in-transit copy of your counter
state (start date, habit, daily spend, currency) is stored on our backend
(Supabase) keyed by a random device identifier. No name, email, or account
is required or collected. You can delete the backup by resetting the app's
data or contacting us.

## Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Google Play. We receive purchase tokens to
verify your subscription status; we do not see or store your payment
details. Google's privacy policy applies to the payment itself.

## What we do NOT collect

- No advertising identifiers, no ad SDKs, no behavioral tracking.
- No location data.
- No contacts, photos, or files.

## Health data

Journal entries and habit data are personal health-related information you
choose to record. They stay on your device (or in your optional backup) and
are never sold, shared, or used for advertising.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

## Changes

We will update this policy with any material change and note the new
effective date above.
