## Name decision (collision check, 2026-10-02)

- **DuoBudget — REJECTED.** Four GitHub repos carry the exact name, including
  `duvindurathnayake/duobudget` (a React Native + Expo couples-budget app, "Designed
  for Two") and `nozeren/duobudget` (Flask couples expense/split app). Same
  category + same stack = genuine confusion risk, the Palate lesson.
- **NestBudget — REJECTED.** Live commercial product (nestbudget web app with
  paid Family plan) plus GitHub repos (`sidmttl/nestbudget` Android family-finance
  app).
- **TwoPurse — ACCEPTED.** No app, product, or Play listing found; only unrelated
  results (purse listings, an eBay username). Fallback PairPurse not needed.
- Claim dir `~/workspace/apps/build-queue/claimed/duobudget/` keeps the queue
  slug; the shipped brand is TwoPurse (recorded here so launch uses the right name).

## Palette (designed from scratch — couples/finance)

Warm terracotta + sage + cream. Money without the cold bank-blue: homey, calm,
trustworthy. Deliberately distinct from Fable (indigo/lavender), Restory
(plum/sand/gold), KeepsFresh (greens/cream), MeetBrief (slate/signal-blue),
Palate (saffron).

| Role | Light | Dark |
|---|---|---|
| Background | cream `#FAF6ED` | deep espresso `#1B140E` |
| Surface | paper `#FFFDF8` | espresso `#241B13` |
| Accent (primary actions) | terracotta `#B85A33` | soft terracotta `#D68A5C` |
| Secondary | sage `#6E7F60` | sage `#98A686` |
| Text | warm ink `#2B2318` | cream `#F3ECDC` |

Envelope colors (8, mid-saturation, readable in both modes): terracotta
`#C1613B`, sage `#7D8C6F`, honey `#D9A441`, dusty rose `#C97B84`, deep teal
`#4E7D7B`, plum `#8A5E8C`, denim `#5E8CB8`, cocoa `#8C6A4A`.

## Voice

Warm, non-judgmental, partnership-first. Never shames spending ("over by X",
not "you blew it"). The money date is framed as a ritual, not a report card.
AR copy mirrors the tone (formal-but-friendly MSA).
