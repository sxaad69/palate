## Full description

Money is the #1 thing couples fight about. TwoPurse ends the fight with a
system built for two: the envelope method, made shared.

**HOW IT WORKS**
- Create envelopes for each spending category — Groceries, Dining, Rent, Fun money — each with a monthly amount.
- Both partners log spending in seconds. The home screen shows every envelope as a "left to spend" bar, not a bank-statement feed.
- Tag each envelope Ours, Mine, or Theirs. Joint envelopes show exactly who spent what — no more "where did it go?"

**THE MONTHLY MONEY DATE**
At month's end, open the Money Date tab together: total spent vs. planned,
your biggest envelope, and a per-person breakdown. One tap rolls leftovers
into Savings and closes the month.

**BUILT FOR REAL COUPLES**
- No accounts, no sign-up, no bank linking — your money data stays on your device.
- Share a plain-text monthly summary with your partner in one tap.
- English + Arabic with full RTL support; USD, EUR, SAR, EGP, AED and more.
- Light & dark themes.

**FREE vs PRO**
Free: 6 envelopes, monthly money date, partner summary sharing.
TwoPurse Pro (weekly subscription with free trial): unlimited envelopes,
money-date reports & trends, CSV export.
