# TwoPurse — Privacy Policy (draft, EN)

**Effective date:** at launch (update before publishing)
**Contact:** via Play Store listing contact email (temp address at launch)

## Summary
TwoPurse is a local-first budgeting app. **We do not collect, transmit, or
store your personal or financial data on any server.** Everything you enter —
envelopes, expenses, names — stays in your device's local storage.

## Data we collect
**None.** The app:
- requires no account and no sign-in;
- does not link to banks or financial institutions;
- sends no analytics, crash reports, or advertising identifiers from the app
  itself (see Third parties for the Play-billing exception);
- works fully offline.

## Data you share yourself
- **Monthly summary sharing:** if you tap "Share summary", the app composes a
  plain-text summary on your device and hands it to Android's share sheet. You
  choose where it goes (e.g., WhatsApp to your partner). We never see it.
- **CSV export:** the CSV is generated on-device and shared only where you send it.

## Third parties
- **Google Play Billing:** if you purchase TwoPurse Pro, the purchase is
  processed by Google Play under Google's privacy policy. The app only learns
  whether the subscription is active; no payment details reach us (there is no
  "us" server at all).

## Data retention & deletion
Your data lives only on your device. Deleting the app (or clearing its data)
permanently deletes everything. There is nothing on a server to request.

## Children
TwoPurse is a general-audience finance tool not directed at children under 13.

## Changes
If a future version adds cloud partner sync (planned v2), this policy will be
updated **before** that version ships, describing exactly what syncs and how to
delete it.
