# Fable — Brand

**Name:** Fable — collision-checked 2026-10-02. "Drift" was taken in-category (Drift 2.0 breathwork on Uptodown; Drift sleep-meditation prototype targeting Android; HoMedics Drift). "Fable" = story, fits narrative breathwork, no breathwork collision found.

**Package:** `com.fable.breath`

**Identity:** night/indigo breath. Deep indigo + night blue surfaces, soft lavender accents, warm ember glow for the breath ring. Dark-first (evening sessions), dawn-soft light mode. Nothing saffron/orange — that palette belongs to Palate (food).

**Voice:** calm, literary, unhurried. Second person, present tense. No clinical language, no medical claims.

**Differentiation vs Breathwrk (Play repetitive-content thresholds):**
1. Story arcs — continuing narratives across sessions (chapters), not isolated exercises.
2. Breath-synced narration — story beats advance with breath phases, not counting.
3. Chapter streaks — narrative continuation as the retention mechanic (next chapter unlocks with streaks).
4. On-device TTS storyteller with guidance density (Full / Cues / Silent) — no streaming, no API cost, offline.

**Icon:** crescent breath-ring in lavender on deep indigo (assets to be generated at launch; adaptive-icon foreground/background placeholders in app.json).
