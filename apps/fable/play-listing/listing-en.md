# Fable — Play listing (EN)

**Title:** Fable: Story Breathwork

**Short description (80 chars):**
Breathe through stories. Guided breathwork with a tale in every session.

**Full description:**
Most breathwork apps count at you. Fable tells you a story.

Every session is a short narrative — a tide house at the water's edge, a dawn climb through pines, a night train through the valley, a rain-warm greenhouse. Your breath sets the pace; the tale unfolds as you inhale and let go. No clinical counting. No repetitive timers.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• 12 story sessions across 4 continuing arcs — each chapter continues the tale
• Breath-synced narration: the story advances with your breath phases
• Chapter streaks: return daily to unlock the next chapter
• On-device storyteller: full narration, gentle cues, or silent with the breath ring
• 4-6 calm breathing, box breathing, 4-7-8 sleep breathing, coherent 5-5
• Streaks, minutes, and session history
• Dark-first design for evening sessions
• English and Arabic, with full RTL support

FREE / PRO
3 chapters are free forever. Fable Pro unlocks all 12 chapters and every future story — weekly plan with a free trial, cancel anytime.

A note: Fable is a wellness companion, not a medical device. Nothing here diagnoses or treats any condition. If you feel dizzy, breathe normally and rest.
