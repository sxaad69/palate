# Privacy Policy — Fable

**Last updated:** 2026-10-02

Fable ("the app") is a story-driven breathwork companion. This policy explains what we collect and why. We collect as little as possible.

## Data we store on your device only
- Session history, streaks, minutes breathed, favorites
- Preferences (language, theme, voice-guidance level, onboarding state)
- Purchase status (Pro unlock)

This data never leaves your phone in v1. There is no account, no analytics SDK, and no advertising SDK.

## Data shared with third parties
- **Google Play Billing:** when you subscribe, Google processes the payment. We receive only the purchase confirmation needed to unlock Pro. Google's privacy policy applies to the transaction.
- **Text-to-speech:** narration uses your device's built-in speech engine. Story text is processed on-device; it is not sent to our servers (we operate none in v1).

## Children's privacy
Fable is not directed at children under 13.

## Your rights
Delete the app to delete all local data. For questions: [support email TBD at launch].

## Changes
We will update this policy if v1's local-only posture changes (e.g., cloud sync). The updated date above will change.
