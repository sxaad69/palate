# Fastuna — Brand Identity

**Name:** Fastuna (fast + tuna? no — fast + "una", one fast, one you. Short, global, ownable.)
**Tagline:** "Fast on your schedule"
**Package:** `com.fastuna.app`

## Palette (designed from scratch for fasting/health — NOT Palate's saffron)

| Role | Light | Dark |
|---|---|---|
| Primary accent | Teal 600 `#0D9488` | Teal 400 `#2DD4BF` |
| Background | Slate 50 `#F8FAFC` (clean) | Deep pine `#0B1F1C` |
| Surface | White | Pine 800 `#143230` |
| Muted accent | Teal 100 `#CCFBF1` | Teal 400 @ 16% |

**Feeling:** vitality, clarity, discipline. Energetic but clean.
**Neutrals:** cool slate (matches teal temperature), never warm stone.

## Icon

A fasting progress ring (~70% arc) in teal on deep pine, with a bright
energy dot at the arc's leading edge. The ring = the fast; the dot = momentum.
Monochrome variant included for themed Android icons.

## Voice

Plain-language, no biohacker jargon. Stages say "Fat burning", not
"lipolysis". Every health claim sits next to a "not medical advice" disclaimer.
