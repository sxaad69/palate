## App name
Fastuna: Fasting Tracker

## Short description (80 chars)

## Full description
Fastuna is the intermittent fasting tracker built for real life — not just the standard 16:8.

**FASTING TIMER**
Start a fast in one tap and watch the live countdown ring. See exactly what stage you're in — settling in, fat burning, deep burn, ketosis — in plain language, no jargon.

**PRESETS FOR REAL SCHEDULES**
- 16:8, 18:6, 20:4, OMAD — the classics
- **Ramadan** — dawn-to-sunset fasting with adjustable suhoor/iftar times
- **Night shift** — an inverted schedule that fasts through daytime sleep
- **Gentle 14:10** — a softer start, popular with women 45+

**STREAKS & ACHIEVEMENTS**
Build a daily streak, earn achievements, and see your total fasts, hours fasted, and completion rate at a glance.

**PRIVATE BY DEFAULT**
Your fasts live on your device. An optional cloud backup keeps them safe.

**BILINGUAL**
Full English and Arabic with right-to-left layout.

Fastuna is a wellness tracker, not medical advice. Consult a professional before fasting, especially if pregnant, nursing, diabetic, or managing a health condition.
