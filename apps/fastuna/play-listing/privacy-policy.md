# Fastuna Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 3, 2026

Fastuna ("the app") is a fasting tracker. This policy explains what data we collect and why.

## Data we collect

**Stored on your device only (never leaves your phone):**
- Your fasting schedule, fast history, streaks, and achievements
- App preferences (language, appearance, onboarding choices)
- Goal selection

**Sent to our servers only if you use cloud backup:**
- Your fast log (start/end times, target hours, preset) — keyed to an anonymous device ID, never to your name, email, or phone number

**If you subscribe to Fastuna Pro:**
- Google Play processes the payment; we never see your card details
- We receive an anonymous purchase token to verify your subscription

## Data we do NOT collect
- No name, email, phone number, or account of any kind
- No location data
- No health data beyond what you enter as fast times
- No advertising identifiers

## Data sharing
We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties. Anonymous fast logs are used only to provide backup/sync.

## Your rights
Delete the app to erase all on-device data. Contact us to request deletion of any server-side backup.

## Children
Fastuna is not directed at children under 13.
