# Fernly — Brand

**Name:** Fernly — like the fern, but for all plants. Chosen after collision checks (Fernly, Sproutly, Growly all clean on Google Play; Leafly rejected — cannabis brand adjacency).

**Identity:** the garden. Fresh leaf green on deep forest — alive, earthy, calm.

**Palette (designed from scratch — NOT Palate's saffron, NOT Fastuna's clinical teal, NOT Pip's sky, NOT Salahly's indigo/gold, NOT Naplet's violet):**
- Primary: leaf green — `#4D7C0F` (light) / `#A3E635` (dark)
- Dark surfaces: deep forest `#060D06` / `#0C1A0C`
- Light surfaces: warm cream `#FAF7F0` / `#FFFFFF`, cool slate neutrals
- Semantic: green `#16A34A`, amber `#D97706`, red `#DC2626`

**Icon:** lime sprout with two leaves over a soil mound, on deep forest.

**The wedge (≥3 differentiators vs Planta/PictureThis):**
1. **Plant Doctor** — rule-based symptom diagnosis (8 diagnoses, 10 symptoms, EN/AR) with treatment advice. The "disease ID" wedge with zero inference cost, offline
2. **Adaptive care schedules** — water/fertilize/mist per species computed from actual logs, with overdue highlighting
3. **Offline-first 12-plant library** — no account, no network needed
4. **Bilingual EN/AR day one** with full RTL — incumbents are English-first

**Package:** `com.fernly.app` · **SKU:** `fernly_pro_weekly` (weekly plan + 7-day free trial, per the deep-dive LTV rule — no monthly-only).
