# Fernly Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 3, 2026

Fernly ("the app") is a plant care reminder and plant diagnosis companion. This policy explains what data we collect and why.

## Data we collect

**Stored on your device only (never leaves your phone):**
- Your plant collection (species, nicknames), care logs, and symptom checks
- App preferences (language, appearance, onboarding choices)

**Sent to our servers only if you use cloud backup:**
- Your plant collection and care logs — keyed to an anonymous device ID, never to your name, email, or phone number

**If you subscribe to Fernly Pro:**
- Google Play processes the payment; we never see your card details
- We receive an anonymous purchase token to verify your subscription

## Data we do NOT collect
- No name, email, phone number, or account of any kind
- No location data, no photos (the Plant Doctor uses symptom checklists, not your camera)
- No advertising identifiers

## Data sharing
We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties. Anonymous logs are used only to provide backup/sync.

## Your rights
Delete the app to erase all on-device data. Contact us to request deletion of any server-side backup.

## Children
Fernly is not directed at children under 13.
