# ThreeGood — Brand

**Name:** **ThreeGood** — collision-checked 2026-10-02. "GlowBack" (proposed)
is TAKEN on Google Play: "GlowBack: GLP-1 Tracker & Diary" (com.mi.glp, active
Sep 2026). Fallback "Grateful" also taken ("Grateful: Gratitude Journal",
com.intertechapps.grateful). "Thankful" was clear but generic; "ThreeGood" is
clear on Play (no gratitude app under that name — only an unrelated B2B
wellbeing site and a tiny "TalkShop by 3Good LLC" comms app) and names the
ritual exactly: the positive-psychology "three good things" exercise. Keep the
claim dir as `19-gratitude` (queue slug); the product is ThreeGood.

**Package:** `com.threegood.app` · **SKU:** `threegood_weekly` (weekly + trial)

**Identity:** sunrise warmth. Morning light over a jar: soft cream backgrounds,
peach-to-gold brand scale, warm-bark neutrals, a literal glow for the jar.
Nothing indigo/lavender (Fable), saffron (Palate), plum (Restory),
terracotta (TwoPurse) or coral/teal (ShadowSay). Light mode is the hero
(morning ritual); dark mode is deep bark, never pure black.

**Voice:** warm, plain, non-cheesy. Short sentences. No toxic positivity, no
clinical claims, no "manifest your dreams".

**Differentiation vs Day One / "Gratitude: Self-Care Journal" / generic journals (Play repetitive-content thresholds):**
1. Ritual-first, not journal-first — the home screen IS today's ritual (3 prompt
   cards, completable in 60 seconds). Past entries are secondary.
2. Rotating gratitude prompts — 60 bundled prompts across 6 themes (people,
   senses, small wins, nature, challenges, wonder), EN+AR; deterministic rotation
   so day 47 never feels like day 1. Free tier unlocks 3 themes; Pro unlocks all 6.
3. Glow jar visualization — completed days fill a visual jar with light; streak
   milestones (7/14/30/60/100/365 days) unlock new jar glow themes. Tangible,
   shareable progress without gamification noise.
4. Weekly reflection letter — the week's gratitudes auto-compiled into a readable
   letter, shareable as text (EN+AR); past letters archived for Pro. A keepsake,
   not a chart.

**Icon:** sunrise (gold circle) rising over a jar silhouette on cream; dark
variant on bark. (Assets to be generated at launch; placeholders in app.json.)

**ASO wedge:** "three good things" is a known exercise (Seligman) with real
search intent; "gratitude ritual" + streaks vs. generic "journal" keywords.
