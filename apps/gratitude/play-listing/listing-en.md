# ThreeGood — Play listing (EN)

**Title:** ThreeGood — Daily Gratitude Journal

**Short description (80 chars):**
Three good things a day. A 60-second gratitude ritual with streaks.

**Full description:**
Most journal apps hand you a blank page. ThreeGood hands you a ritual.

Every day you get three fresh gratitude prompts — real ones, not "what are
you grateful for?" on repeat — drawn from 60 prompts across six themes:
people, senses, small wins, nature, challenges, and wonder. Answer all three
in about sixty seconds and the day is done. Your glow jar fills with light.
Keep the streak and unlock new jar themes at 7, 14, 30, 60, 100 and 365 days.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Ritual-first home: today's 3 prompts, completable in 60 seconds
• 60 rotating prompts across 6 themes — day 47 never feels like day 1
• Glow jar: a living visualization of your streak, with unlockable glow themes
• Streaks that reward showing up, not perfection
• Weekly reflection letter: your week's gratitudes written back to you,
  shareable as text — a keepsake, not a chart
• Daily reminder at a time you choose
• Light and dark themes, English and Arabic with full RTL support
• 100% private: everything stays on your phone

FREE / PRO
The daily ritual, 3 prompt themes, this week's history, and your weekly letter
are free forever. ThreeGood Pro unlocks unlimited history, all 6 prompt
themes, the weekly letter archive, and full journal export — weekly plan with
a free trial, cancel anytime.

A note: ThreeGood is a wellness companion, not a medical device. Nothing here
diagnoses or treats any condition.
