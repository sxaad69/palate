FULL DESCRIPTION
Your attention is the product. Guard it.

HeadDown is a focus timer with a distraction guard — honest about what a
phone app can and can't do.

FOCUS SESSIONS
25, 50, or 90 minutes (custom on Plus). Label what you're working on,
start the timer, head down.

THE DISTRACTION GUARD
Drift to another app mid-session and it's detected and logged as a
distraction — with a timestamp. No hiding from yourself.

FOCUS SCORE
Every session gets a score: focused minutes ÷ total. Watch it climb with
streaks, 7-day charts, and total deep-work hours.

STRICT MODE (Plus)
Three app exits break the session. Real stakes for real focus.

SESSION DEBRIEF
End with a debrief: your score, when you drifted, and what you got done.

Private by design: everything lives on your device. No account needed.
