# Privacy Policy — HeadDown

**Effective date:** [launch date] · **Contact:** [support email]

HeadDown ("the app") is a focus timer with distraction logging. This
policy explains what data we handle and why.

## Data stored on your device

Your session data — focus sessions, distraction logs, reflections,
settings — is stored locally on your device using the app's private
storage. It never leaves your device unless you enable cloud backup (see
below).

## Optional cloud backup

If cloud backup is enabled, an encrypted-in-transit copy of your sessions
is stored on our backend (Supabase) keyed by a random device identifier. No
name, email, or account is required or collected. You can delete the backup
by erasing the app's data or contacting us.

## Notifications

Session-end reminders are scheduled locally on your device. Reminder
content never leaves your device.

## Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Google Play. We receive purchase tokens to
verify your subscription status; we do not see or store your payment
details. Google's privacy policy applies to the payment itself.

## What we do NOT collect

- No advertising identifiers, no ad SDKs, no behavioral tracking.
- No location data.
- No contacts, photos, or files.
- We do NOT monitor which apps you use — the distraction guard only
  detects when you leave HeadDown itself, never what you open next.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

## Changes

We will update this policy with any material change and note the new
effective date above.
