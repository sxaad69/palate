# Hulm — Brand

Dream journal & reflection (bench #1). Claimed as `claimed/bench-dream`.

**Name note:** "Hulm" (حلم — "dream" in Arabic) is clear — no app
collisions. Bilingual by nature: the brand itself is EN/AR. Package:
`com.hulm.app`.

## Identity

**Midnight plum + moonlit amber.** Deep night violet, warm amber glow,
soft mist. Dreamlike, calm, honest — a journal, not a fortune-teller.
Nothing like the other apps' palettes.

## Voice

Gentle, reflective, humble. Short sentences. "Dreams are for reflecting,
not for ruling."

## Concept (the honest architecture)

A dream journal with a **symbol lexicon** — explicitly NOT an AI oracle
and NOT a religious authority:

1. **Journal** — log dreams (narrative, mood, date). Streaks for
   consistency.
2. **Symbols** — the app matches your words against a bundled lexicon
   (~48 symbols, EN/AR keywords). YOU confirm which fit.
3. **Reflect** — each symbol offers themes + reflective questions, plus a
   one-line "traditional association" clearly labeled as cultural
   tradition, not scholarship.
4. **Patterns** — recurring symbols across your journal (Plus).

The disclaimer is structural, not a footnote: shown in onboarding, in the
reflection view, and in the listing. "For reflection and journaling — not
religious rulings (tafsir al-ahlam). Not a substitute for scholarly
interpretation."

## Differentiation vs Dream Moods / DreamApp / myislamicdream

1. **Bilingual EN/AR day one** with full RTL — dream interpretation is
   culturally huge in the Arab world; incumbents are English-first or
   web-only.
2. **Reflection framing** — journaling + prompts, honest about limits,
   instead of fake-authoritative "your dream means X".
3. **Traditional associations labeled as tradition** — respectful, not
   pretending to be Ibn Sirin.
4. **Recurring-symbol detection** across the journal (Plus).

## Honesty notes

- Keyword matching is crude by design — the app says the human decides.
- No AI, no cloud, no accounts. Everything on-device.
