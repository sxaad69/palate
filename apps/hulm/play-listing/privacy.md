# Privacy Policy — Hulm

_Last updated: 2026-10-03_

Hulm ("we", "our", "the app") is a dream journaling app. Your privacy matters,
and this app is designed to be private by default.

## Data we store
- **Your dream entries** (titles, narratives, moods, symbol confirmations) are
  stored locally on your device using on-device storage.
- **Preferences** (language, appearance theme) are stored locally on your device.

## Optional cloud backup
If you enable sync (a future feature; not in v1), your dream entries may be
backed up to our Supabase database keyed by a random device identifier. The
backup contains only the same content you already store locally. We do not
sell, rent, or share your data with third parties.

## Purchases
Plus subscriptions are processed by Google Play. We receive only the standard
purchase status (active/expired) from Google Play Billing; we do not see your
payment details.

## Analytics & advertising
We do not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs. We do not track you
across apps or websites.

## Children
The app is not directed at children under 13.

## Deletion
You can erase all your dream data at any time from Settings → Erase all data.
Uninstalling the app also removes all locally stored data.

## Contact
For privacy questions: use the support link on the Google Play listing.
