# KeepsFresh — Brand

**App:** KeepsFresh — pantry & expiry tracker (Portfolio App #10, Utilities/Lifestyle)
**Package:** `com.keepsfresh.pantry`
**Subscription SKU (create at launch):** `keepsfresh_weekly` — weekly + free trial (deep-dive LTV rule)

## Name decision (collision check 2026-10-02)

"KeepsFresh" searched on the general web and Google Play:

- **No app on Google Play named KeepsFresh.** No live collision.
- Only hits: "KEEPSFRESH LLP" — a defunct, struck-off Singapore grocery retailer (closed; unrelated category, no app), and `com.rii.keepfresh` — an APK-mirror listing for an app **not on the Play Store** (removed/unpublished).
- Fallbacks if a conflict ever appears: **PantryPal**, **UseBy**, **FreshLedger**.

**Decision: keep "KeepsFresh".** Descriptive for the ASO keywords (keeps food fresh), no trademark/app collision found.

## Visual identity

Designed from scratch for the kitchen/pantry domain — **fresh herb greens + warm cream**. Deliberately distinct from sibling apps:

| App | Palette |
|---|---|
| Fable (breathwork) | deep indigo / lavender |
| Restory | plum / sand / gold |
| Palate | saffron |
| **KeepsFresh** | **leaf green / warm cream / ripe-amber urgency** |

- **Primary:** leaf green scale (`green500–700`), cream paper neutrals (warm, never cool grays).
- **Urgency accent:** ripe amber-orange (`ripe500`) for "use soon" — produce-aisle ripeness metaphor.
- **Dark mode:** deep green-ink (`green950`), surfaces lighten with elevation, accent lightened ~15% to avoid vibration.
- **Typography:** system font, 1.25 modular scale, 3 weights max.
- **Iconography:** emoji glyphs (🧺 ⏳ 📊 ⚙️) — keeps v1 dependency-free, matches portfolio convention.

## Voice

Practical, kitchen-table friendly. No breathwork calm, no finance jargon. "Use soon, not too late."
