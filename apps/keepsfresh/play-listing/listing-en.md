## Full description

Stop throwing food away. KeepsFresh is the expiry date tracker that tells you what to use *before* it spoils.

**USE SOON QUEUE — NOT JUST A DATE LIST**
Most expiry apps give you a boring list of dates. KeepsFresh ranks your food by waste risk — days left × quantity × how perishable it is — so the chicken that expires tomorrow jumps above the rice that lasts a year.

**SMART EXPIRY DATES**
Type "milk" and KeepsFresh pre-fills 7 days in the fridge from a built-in database of 60+ common foods. No more guessing use-by dates.

**WASTE-SAVED COUNTER**
Every item you use up before it spoils adds to your running total of money saved. Watch your weekly waste report shrink.

**EXPIRY REMINDERS**
Get a local notification before food expires — no account, no cloud, everything stays on your device.

**KEY FEATURES**
- Expiry date tracker for pantry, fridge & freezer
- "Use soon" urgency queue sorted by waste-risk score
- 60+ food database with smart shelf-life defaults
- Waste-saved money counter + weekly waste report
- Local expiry reminders (use-by date alerts)
- Pantry organizer with search, categories & quantities
- Works fully offline — your data never leaves your phone
- English & Arabic with full RTL support

Whether you call it a food waste app, a pantry organizer, or a use-by date reminder — KeepsFresh keeps your groceries fresh and your wallet fuller.

**Pro (optional weekly subscription with free trial):** unlimited items, weekly waste reports, pantry CSV export. Free forever for up to 30 items.
