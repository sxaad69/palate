# Privacy Policy — KeepsFresh

**Last updated:** October 2, 2026

## Local-only data posture

KeepsFresh is designed to work **entirely on your device**. Your pantry items, expiry dates, quantities, waste logs, settings, and language preferences are stored locally on your phone (using on-device storage) and are **never uploaded to our servers** — because we don't operate any servers for this app.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal data, usage analytics, or advertising identifiers.

## Third-party services

- **Google Play Billing** — only if you purchase the optional Pro subscription. Payment processing is handled entirely by Google under the [Google Play Terms of Service](https://play.google.com/about/play-terms/). We never see your payment details.
- **No analytics, no ads, no crash reporting SDKs** are included in this version of the app.

## Notifications

Expiry reminders are scheduled **locally on your device** using the operating system's notification service. No reminder data leaves your phone.

## Data deletion

Because all data lives on your device, you can delete everything at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

## Children

KeepsFresh is not directed at children under 13, and we knowingly collect no data from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes (for example, if a future version adds optional cloud sync), we will update this document and the app's Data Safety section before the change takes effect.

## Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer via the Play Store listing's contact email.
