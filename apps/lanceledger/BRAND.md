# LanceLedger — Brand

Freelancer expense tracker with client profitability. App #6 of the portfolio
(queue order; claimed as `expensewise`).

**Name note:** "ExpenseWise" is heavily taken (multiple GitHub projects);
"SoloLedger" is taken (multiple products); "GigProfit" is a live Play Store
app (`app.gigprofit`). **LanceLedger** is free — no app collisions found.
Package: `com.lanceledger.app`.

## Identity

**Ledger — money, grown up.** Deep pine green on warm paper. Trustworthy,
quiet, precise — the opposite of neon fintech. Profit glows mint; losses are
honest red. Nothing like Palate's saffron or DrySpell's dawn.

## Voice

Plain-spoken, freelancer-to-freelancer. "Know your number." No accounting
jargon; every figure explains itself. Standing disclaimer: not tax advice —
talk to a qualified professional.

## Differentiation vs Wave / Zoho (free incumbents)

1. **Per-client profitability** — income vs expenses per client, ranked. "Which
   clients actually make you money?" Wave/Zoho are accounting-first; this is
   freelancer-lens.
2. **Tax shield** — auto-computed set-aside from real profit × your tax rate,
   so quarterly estimates stop being a panic.
3. **Deductible flagging + one-tap tax report** — mark deductible expenses,
   export a CSV your accountant will love.
4. **AR/EN RTL bilingual** — the free incumbents are weak on Arabic.
