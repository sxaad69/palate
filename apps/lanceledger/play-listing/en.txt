Know your number.

LanceLedger is the dead-simple finance tracker built for freelancers,
solopreneurs, and gig workers — not accountants.

TRACK INCOME & EXPENSES IN SECONDS
Log an expense or income in under 10 seconds: amount, category, client,
done. No chart of accounts, no accounting degree required.

WHICH CLIENTS MAKE YOU MONEY?
Tag transactions with a client and LanceLedger ranks them by real profit —
income minus expenses. Stop guessing which work is worth it.

TAX SHIELD
Set your tax rate once. LanceLedger computes a running set-aside from your
real profit, so quarterly estimates stop being a panic. You'll always know
exactly how much to move to your tax savings.

DEDUCTIBLE EXPENSES, ONE-TAP REPORT
Flag deductible business expenses as you go. At tax time, export a clean
CSV report your accountant will love — no shoebox, no scramble.

MONTHLY P&L & CATEGORY BREAKDOWN
See this month's profit at a glance and exactly where the money went, by
category.

Private by design: your data lives on your device, with optional encrypted
cloud backup. No account needed.

LanceLedger is a record-keeping tool, not tax advice. Talk to a qualified
professional about your taxes.
