# Privacy Policy — LanceLedger

**Effective date:** [launch date] · **Contact:** [support email]

LanceLedger ("the app") is a finance tracker for freelancers. This policy
explains what data we handle and why.

## Data stored on your device

Your financial data — transactions, clients, currency, tax rate — is stored
locally on your device using the app's private storage. It never leaves your
device unless you enable cloud backup (see below).

## Optional cloud backup

If cloud backup is enabled, an encrypted-in-transit copy of your ledger
(transactions and clients) is stored on our backend (Supabase) keyed by a
random device identifier. No name, email, or account is required or
collected. You can delete the backup by erasing the app's data or contacting
us.

## Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Google Play. We receive purchase tokens to
verify your subscription status; we do not see or store your payment
details. Google's privacy policy applies to the payment itself.

## What we do NOT collect

- No advertising identifiers, no ad SDKs, no behavioral tracking.
- No location data.
- No contacts, photos, or files.

## Financial data

Transaction and client data is sensitive personal information you choose to
record. It stays on your device (or in your optional backup) and is never
sold, shared, or used for advertising.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

## Changes

We will update this policy with any material change and note the new
effective date above.
