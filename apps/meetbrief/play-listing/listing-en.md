## Full description
Meetings end. Action items shouldn't get lost with them.

MeetBrief is a meeting recorder built for freelancers, students, and small
teams who can't justify Otter.ai or Fireflies — and don't want their
conversations living on someone else's cloud.

**RECORD WITHOUT THE CLOUD**
Hit record and MeetBrief captures high-quality audio right on your phone.
No meeting bots, no calendar invites, no account. Works fully offline.

**ACTION ITEMS, NOT WALLS OF TEXT**
Every meeting ends with a checkable action list front and center — add,
edit, and tick off follow-ups so nothing slips.

**MEETING COST TIMER**
Enter an hourly rate and watch what the meeting costs as it runs. A
47-minute sync at $80/hr is $62.67 — suddenly the recap writes itself.

**INSTANT SHARE PACK**
One tap copies a neatly formatted recap — summary, action items, notes —
ready to paste into WhatsApp or email, in English or Arabic.

**PRIVATE BY DESIGN**
Recordings, notes, and action items stay on your device. MeetBrief has no
account system and sends your audio nowhere. Delete a meeting and its
recording is gone for good.

**FREE PLAN**
5 meetings per month, recordings up to 30 minutes. MeetBrief Pro
(weekly subscription with free trial) unlocks unlimited meetings and
longer recordings.

MeetBrief is an indie app. Your meetings stay yours.
