# Privacy Policy — MeetBrief

**Last updated: 2026-10-02**

MeetBrief ("the app") is designed so that your data never leaves your
device.

## Data we do NOT collect
- We operate **no account system**. We do not ask for your name, email, or
  any identifier.
- We run **no analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs**.
- The app makes **no network requests** in its current version.

## Data stored on your device only
- **Meeting recordings** — audio files are written to the app's private
  document directory on your device. They are never uploaded, streamed,
  or shared by the app.
- **Meeting metadata** — titles, durations, notes, transcripts you paste,
  action items, and hourly rates are stored in on-device app storage.
- **Preferences** — language, appearance, and subscription status are
  stored on-device.

## Microphone
The app requests microphone access solely to record meetings when you tap
the record button. Recording happens locally; audio never leaves the device.

## Deletion
Deleting a meeting inside the app permanently deletes its recording and
all associated notes from the device. Uninstalling the app removes all
app data.

## Subscriptions
If you purchase MeetBrief Pro, payment is processed entirely by Google
Play. We do not see or store your payment details.

## Children
The app is not directed at children under 13, and we knowingly collect no
data from anyone.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the updated version will ship inside the app and
be reflected here before the update reaches users.

## Contact
For privacy questions, contact the developer through the Google Play
listing's contact channel.
