# ModestFit — Brand

**Name:** ModestFit — collision-checked 2026-10-02. Web search found no Google Play app named "ModestFit" (results were: Modanisa shopping app, Zinisa modest-fashion store, hijab photo-frame spam apps, a "ModeFit" survey form, and modestfit.online — a small web-only clothing retailer, not an app). Fallbacks on standby: HijabStyle, CoverChic, ModestMuse. The queue verdict's named competitors are ALIFF and Fashion Frame (both virtual-try-on apps — a lane we deliberately do NOT enter in v1).

**Package:** `com.modestfit.app`

**Identity:** earthy elegance. Warm sand + olive + soft rose — a palette designed from scratch for modest fashion (NOT reused from any sibling; nothing indigo/night like Fable, nothing saffron like Palate). Olive is the primary brand hue (growth, calm, modest earthiness); soft rose is the secondary accent (hijab pairing moments, highlights); warm sand/ivory surfaces keep it light and breathable. Dark mode is warm charcoal, never pure black.

**Voice:** warm, practical, respectful. Second person, plain language. No fashion-snob jargon, no body commentary — the app talks about clothes and plans, never bodies.

**Differentiation vs ALIFF / Fashion Frame / generic wardrobe apps (Play repetitive-content thresholds):**
1. Modesty-first data model — every piece carries coverage attributes (sleeve length, hem length, opacity); the outfit checker flags combinations that miss the user's own minimums. No competitor does this systematically.
2. Hijab pairing engine — every outfit/suggestion gets hijab picks from the user's own hijab collection via local color-harmony rules (no AI, no API, zero cost).
3. Occasion + 7-day planning — outfits planned per day with occasion tags (daily, work, wedding, travel, evening); weather stays manual in v1 (documented as v2).
4. Capsule wardrobe insights — "12 pieces, 28 outfits" efficiency score plus most-worn / never-worn stats.

**Icon:** olive hijab-knot / folded-abaya mark on warm sand (assets to be generated at launch; adaptive-icon foreground/background placeholders in app.json).

**Scope note (queue verdict):** NO virtual try-on in v1 — that's AR/camera territory and explicitly out of scope. We race ALIFF/Fashion Frame on planning + modesty intelligence, not on try-on.
