# ModestFit — Play listing (EN)

**Title:** ModestFit: Modest Outfit Planner

**Short description (80 chars):**
Plan modest outfits from your own wardrobe — hijab pairing included.

**Full description:**
Getting dressed should be easy — and always feel right. ModestFit is the wardrobe and outfit planner built for modest dressers.

Catalog your abayas, hijabs, skirts, trousers and tops, then plan your week by occasion. ModestFit checks every outfit against YOUR modesty preferences and pairs the perfect hijab from your own collection.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Modest wardrobe catalog — abayas, hijabs, skirts, trousers, tops, dresses, outerwear, with your own photos
• Modesty checker — set your minimums (sleeve length, hem length, opacity) and every outfit is checked against them
• Hijab pairing engine — color-harmony suggestions from the hijabs you actually own
• 7-day outfit planner with occasion tags: daily, work, wedding, travel, evening
• Shop-your-closet ideas — outfit combos generated from your own pieces, never a store
• Wardrobe insights — most-worn pieces, never-worn pieces, and your capsule score ("12 pieces, 28 outfits")
• 40-piece starter catalog to begin instantly
• English and Arabic, with full RTL support

FREE / PRO
The free plan holds 20 wardrobe pieces. ModestFit Pro unlocks unlimited pieces, the 7-day planner, and wardrobe insights — weekly plan with a free trial, cancel anytime.

A note: ModestFit stores everything on your device. Your wardrobe photos never leave your phone.
