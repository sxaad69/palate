# Privacy Policy — ModestFit

**Last updated:** 2026-10-02

ModestFit ("the app") is a modest wardrobe and outfit planner. This policy explains what we collect and why. We collect as little as possible.

## Data we store on your device only
- Wardrobe pieces, photos, outfits, planner entries, wear counts
- Preferences (language, theme, modesty preferences, onboarding state)
- Purchase status (Pro unlock)

This data never leaves your phone in v1. There is no account, no analytics SDK, and no advertising SDK. Wardrobe photos are stored in the app's private storage and are never uploaded anywhere.

## Data shared with third parties
- **Google Play Billing:** when you subscribe, Google processes the payment. We receive only the purchase confirmation needed to unlock Pro. Google's privacy policy applies to the transaction.

## Children's privacy
ModestFit is not directed at children under 13.

## Your rights
Delete the app to delete all local data. For questions: [support email TBD at launch].

## Changes
We will update this policy if v1's local-only posture changes (e.g., cloud sync). The updated date above will change.
