# Restory — Brand

**Name:** Restory — collision-checked 2026-10-02. "Drowse" (sleep calculator + white-noise apps), "Nightly" (Renight sleep sounds), "Moonlog" (baby sleep tracker, iOS lifestyle), "Hushly" (baby sleep apps), "Sloom" (baby feeding app, EEG sleep platform, sleep gummies) all collide in the sleep space. "Restory" has no sleep-journal app collision (only restory.in, an Indian health platform, and restory-health.com grounding sheets — different space). rest + story: your sleep writes your days.

**Package:** `com.restory.app`

**Identity:** twilight journal. Twilight plum depth for dark mode (evening journaling), warm sand light mode (morning), soft gold accents like lamplight. Nothing indigo/lavender — that palette belongs to Fable (breathwork). Nothing saffron/orange — that belongs to Palate (food). Every app gets its own palette.

**Voice:** quiet, warm, a little literary. Plain-language, no clinical claims. "Your sleep writes your days."

**Differentiation vs Daylio (Play repetitive-content thresholds):**
1. Sleep-first flow — the check-in starts with last night's sleep (quality, bed/wake times) and pairs it with today's mood automatically. Daylio is mood-first; sleep is an afterthought activity tag.
2. Mood↔sleep correlation insights — "You rate days 1.4 points higher after 4★+ sleep", your sweet-spot bedtime and sleep length, computed on-device from the user's own data.
3. Bedtime wind-down nudge — a gentle daily local reminder tied to the user's target bedtime, opening a 20-second "close the day" ritual.
4. Weekly "rest story" — a plain-language narrative of the week's sleep-mood pattern, generated locally from templates (EN+AR), not an LLM call.

**Icon:** crescent moon in soft gold on twilight plum (assets to be generated at launch; adaptive-icon foreground/background placeholders in app.json).
