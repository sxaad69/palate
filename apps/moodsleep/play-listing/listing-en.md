# Restory — Play listing (EN)

**Title:** Restory: Sleep & Mood Journal

**Short description (80 chars):**
Sleep-first mood journal. Log your night, see how it shapes your day.

**Full description:**
Daylio made micro-journaling famous — but it treats sleep as an afterthought. Restory starts where your day really starts: last night.

THE SLEEP-FIRST CHECK-IN (under 30 seconds)
• Log last night's sleep first — quality, bedtime, wake time
• One tap for today's mood, one optional line about the day
• No forms, no friction: open, tap, done

THE MOOD↔SLEEP CONNECTION
• See how your sleep quality drives your next-day mood — from your own data
• "You rate days 1.4 points higher after 4-star sleep" — computed on your phone, never uploaded
• Your sweet spot: the bedtime and sleep length behind your best nights
• 7-day mood and sleep charts

YOUR WEEKLY REST STORY
• Every week, a plain-language narrative of your sleep-mood pattern
• Written from your data by the app — no account, no cloud

BEDTIME WIND-DOWN NUDGE
• A gentle evening reminder tied to your target bedtime
• Opens a 20-second "close the day" ritual

FREE / PRO
Free: full check-in, 7-day history. Restory Pro unlocks unlimited history, the sleep↔mood correlation insights, weekly rest stories, and CSV export — weekly plan with a free trial, cancel anytime.

PRIVACY
Everything stays on your device. No account, no servers, no analytics. Your journal is yours.

A note: Restory is a wellness companion, not a medical device. Nothing here diagnoses or treats any condition.
