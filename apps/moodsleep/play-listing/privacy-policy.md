# Privacy Policy — Restory

**Last updated:** 2026-10-02

Restory ("the app") is a sleep and mood micro-journal. This policy describes what the app does with your information.

## Data we collect

**None.** Restory stores your journal entries (mood, sleep quality, bed/wake times, notes) exclusively in your device's local storage. The app:

- requires no account and no sign-in,
- sends no data to any server,
- includes no analytics SDK and no advertising SDK,
- transmits nothing over the network except subscription purchases through Google Play Billing.

## Purchases

If you subscribe to Restory Pro, payment is processed entirely by Google Play Billing under Google's own privacy terms. The app does not see or store your payment details; it only learns whether the subscription is active so it can unlock Pro features.

## Notifications

The optional bedtime reminder is a local notification scheduled on your device. No information leaves your device to deliver it.

## Data deletion

Because all data lives on your device, uninstalling the app permanently deletes your journal. There is no server-side copy to request deletion of.

## Children

Restory is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will ship with the app and the "Last updated" date above will change.

## Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer through the Play Store listing.
