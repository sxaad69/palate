# Naplet — Brand

**Name:** Naplet — a little nap. Chosen after collision checks (Nod taken twice, Lulla taken, Ninni crowded, Snoo is the smart-bassinet brand; Naplet clean on Google Play).

**Identity:** 3am. Soft violet moonlight on deep plum night — calm, dim, gentle. The app should feel like a nightlight, not a dashboard.

**Palette (designed from scratch — NOT Palate's saffron, NOT Fastuna's teal, NOT Pip's sky, NOT Salahly's indigo/gold):**
- Primary: soft violet — `#7C3AED` (light) / `#A78BFA` (dark)
- Dark surfaces: deep plum `#141122` / `#1E1B2E` (night mode)
- Light surfaces: warm cream `#FDF9F3` / `#FFFFFF`, cool slate neutrals
- Semantic: green `#16A34A`, amber `#D97706`, red `#DC2626`, violet info

**Icon:** violet crescent moon with small stars on deep plum.

**The wedge (≥3 differentiators vs Baby Tracker/Huckleberry/Nod):**
1. **3am night mode** — giant one-thumb log buttons, dim colors, system dark theme. Designed for exhausted one-handed use in the dark
2. **Sleep↔feeding insight cards** — simple on-device correlations (longest stretch, feed intervals, early-bedtime pattern)
3. **Family sync via short code** — no accounts; every caregiver phone shares one timeline (Supabase, last-write-wins)
4. **Bilingual EN/AR day one** with full RTL — incumbents are English-first

**Package:** `com.naplet.app` · **SKU:** `naplet_pro_weekly` (weekly plan + 7-day free trial, per the deep-dive LTV rule — no monthly-only).
