# Naplet Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 3, 2026

Naplet ("the app") is a baby sleep and feeding tracker. This policy explains what data we collect and why.

## Data we collect

**Stored on your device only (never leaves your phone):**
- Your baby's name, your logging events (sleep, feeds, diapers), and notes
- App preferences (language, units, appearance, onboarding choices)

**Sent to our servers only if you set a family sync code:**
- Your logging events — keyed to the family code you choose, never to your name, email, or phone number. Anyone with the code can read and write the shared timeline: only share it with people you trust.

**If you subscribe to Naplet Pro:**
- Google Play processes the payment; we never see your card details
- We receive an anonymous purchase token to verify your subscription

## Data we do NOT collect
- No name, email, phone number, or account of any kind
- No location data
- No advertising identifiers

## Data sharing
We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties. Family-synced events are visible only to devices using the same family code.

## Your rights
Delete the app to erase all on-device data. Contact us to request deletion of any server-side family data.

## Children
Naplet is a parenting tool for adults; it is not directed at children under 13. The baby's name you enter stays on your device unless you enable family sync.
