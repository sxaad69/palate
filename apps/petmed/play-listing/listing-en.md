## Full description

Never miss a dose for your best friend.

Pawscript is the calm, friendly home for your pets' health: medications with on-time reminders, vaccination records with due-date alerts, a weight log, and a vet-visit diary — for one pet or a whole furry family.

**All your pets, one timeline**
The Doses tab shows every due dose across ALL your pets in a single timeline, color-coded per pet. No more flipping between profiles or mental math.

**Tap "Given" — watch the streaks grow**
Log each dose with one tap. Pawscript tracks weekly adherence % per medication and streaks per pet — borrowed from the best human med apps, rare in pet apps.

**Walk into the vet prepared**
One tap compiles a vet-visit prep sheet: current meds, adherence, weight trend, vaccination status, recent visits and notes. Share it as text with your vet. No more "I think it was last month…"

**Weight + photo timeline**
Log weight with a simple trend view, and keep a photo on each pet's profile (stored on your device only).

**Everything else**
- Vaccination list with overdue / due-soon alerts
- Vet visit log: date, clinic, reason, notes
- Local dose reminders — works offline
- English + Arabic, light + dark mode

**Free & Pro**
Free: 1 pet with full dose tracking and reminders.
Pawscript Pro (weekly plan with free trial, cancel anytime): unlimited pets, vet-visit prep sheets, export.

**Privacy by design**
All your pets' data stays on your device. No account needed. No ads.

Pawscript reminds; it does not prescribe. Always follow your vet's instructions.
