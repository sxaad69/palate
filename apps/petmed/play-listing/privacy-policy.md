# Privacy Policy — Pawscript

**Last updated:** 2026-10-02

Pawscript ("the app") is built privacy-first: **your pet data never leaves your device.**

## Data we collect
**None.** We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on any server. There is no account system, no analytics, no crash reporting, and no advertising SDK in the app.

## Data stored on your device
The app stores the following locally on your device (AsyncStorage + app document directory), solely so the app can function:

- Pet profiles you create (name, species, breed, birthdate, notes, photo you choose)
- Medication schedules, dose logs, weight entries
- Vaccination records and vet-visit logs
- App preferences (language, appearance, subscription status)

Pet photos are copied into the app's private document directory on your device. Local dose-reminder notifications are scheduled on-device via the OS notification system.

## Data sharing
We share nothing with third parties. There are no third-party SDKs that receive data.

## Purchases
If you purchase Pawscript Pro, payment is processed by Google Play. We receive only the entitlement status (Pro active/inactive) needed to unlock features. We do not receive or store your payment details.

## Children
The app is not directed at children under 13 and contains no user-generated public content.

## Deletion
Use **Profile → Erase all data** inside the app to delete everything stored on your device. Uninstalling the app also removes all local data. Pet photos are deleted with the app's document directory.

## Changes
If a future version adds optional cloud sync, this policy will be updated and the sync will be opt-in.

## Contact
For privacy questions, contact the developer through the Play Store listing.

---

**Disclaimer:** Pawscript is a reminder and record-keeping tool. It does not provide veterinary advice, diagnosis, or prescriptions. Always follow your veterinarian's instructions.
