# Pip — Brand

**Name:** Pip — a tiny seed. Small behaviors, big change. Chosen after collision checks (Tinywin, Anchorly, Shine, Pearl, Tinify, Afterly, Maui, Acorn all rejected); no habit tracker named Pip on Google Play.

**Identity:** clear-morning sky. Optimistic, calm, fresh — the opposite of guilt-driven habit apps.

**Palette (designed from scratch, sky/navy — NOT Palate's saffron, NOT Fastuna's teal, NOT Loop's teal-green):**
- Primary: sky blue — `#0284C7` (light) / `#38BDF8` (dark)
- Dark surfaces: deep navy `#0A1628` / `#12233A`
- Light surfaces: `#F7FAFC` / `#FFFFFF`, cool slate neutrals
- Semantic: green `#16A34A`, amber `#D97706`, red `#DC2626`

**Icon:** white seed ("pip") tilted 24°, rising from a sky-blue ring with a small sprout dot — on deep navy.

**Method:** the Tiny Habits method (BJ Fogg), made structural: every habit is a *recipe* ("After I [anchor], I will [behavior]") with a *celebration*. The app's three differentiators are the method's three pillars:
1. **Recipe builder** — anchors + tiny behaviors, not free-text checklist
2. **Shine moment** — post-check-in celebration ritual with a picker
3. **Shrink coach** — miss 2+ days → Fogg's debugging rule suggests a tinier behavior

**Package:** `com.pip.app` · **SKU:** `pip_pro_weekly` (weekly plan + 7-day free trial, per the deep-dive LTV rule — no monthly-only).
