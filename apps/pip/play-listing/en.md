# Pip — tiny habits, done daily

Build big change from tiny behaviors. Pip is a tiny-habits tracker built on the Tiny Habits method: anchor a tiny behavior to an existing routine, then celebrate immediately. No 30-day challenges, no guilt — just recipes so small you can't say no.

WHY PIP IS DIFFERENT
• Recipe builder, not a checklist — every habit is written as "After I [anchor], I will [tiny behavior]," because habits need a home
• Shine moment — a post-check-in celebration ritual that makes the habit stick (the part every other tracker skips)
• Shrink coach — miss 2 days and Pip suggests making the behavior tinier instead of shaming you
• Anchor library — proven daily routines to attach your habits to

FEATURES
• Start with 6 proven starter recipes or build your own in seconds
• Streaks, win history, and a weekly dots view
• 100% local-first — your habits stay on your device
• English + Arabic with full RTL support
• Light & dark themes

Optional Pip Pro (weekly plan, 7-day free trial): unlimited recipes, shrink coach, full win history, anchor library.

Small behaviors. Big change.
