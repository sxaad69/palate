**Full description:**

Your desk is wrecking your posture. StraightUp measures it — honestly.

**HOW IT WORKS**
1. Take a side-profile photo with the guided camera (alignment lines show you exactly how to stand).
2. Drag 5 markers onto your ear, shoulder, hip, knee and ankle.
3. Get a real posture score (0–100) computed from your markers: forward-head angle, shoulder lean, knee bend and hip shift — with the full formula shown in the app. No fake "AI score", no black box.

**FIX WHAT'S ACTUALLY OFF**
Every weak angle gets targeted exercises, not generic advice:
- Forward head → chin tucks, neck stretches
- Rounded shoulders → wall angels, doorway chest stretch
- Hips pushed forward → glute bridges, hip-flexor stretches
12 desk-posture exercises with clear instructions, in English and Arabic.

**TRACK PROGRESS**
Timeline of every check with photos, angle history and side-by-side comparison against your previous check. Watch your score climb.

**STAY UNHUNCHED**
Gentle local reminders during the day ("roll your shoulders back") — scheduled on your phone, nothing uploaded.

**PRIVATE BY DESIGN**
Photos are analyzed on your device and never leave it. No account, no cloud, no ads, no tracking.

**HONEST SCORING**
StraightUp v1 does not use automatic pose-detection AI — you place the markers, the app does transparent geometry. The scoring formula is shown with every result.

StraightUp is a wellness tool, not a medical device. Scores and exercises are for general awareness and are not a diagnosis. See a clinician for pain or injury.
