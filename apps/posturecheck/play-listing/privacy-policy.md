# Privacy Policy — StraightUp

**Effective date:** October 2026

StraightUp ("the app") is built to keep your data on your device.

## Data we collect
**None.** StraightUp does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data.

- **Photos:** Posture photos you capture or pick are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. They are analyzed on-device and never uploaded anywhere.
- **Check history & exercise log:** Stored locally on your device (AsyncStorage). Deleted when you delete a check or uninstall the app.
- **Camera:** Used only to capture your posture photo, with your permission. You can use the gallery picker instead.
- **Notifications:** Posture reminders are scheduled locally on your device. No push service, no account, no server.
- **Purchases:** Subscription purchases are processed by Google Play. We do not see or store your payment details.

## Children's privacy
The app is a general wellness tool not directed at children under 13, and collects no data from anyone.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the updated version will ship with the app.
