# SalahMate — Brand

Prayer-time + habit companion. App #9 of the portfolio (queue order; claimed
as `prayerhabit`).

**Name note:** "PrayerPal" is taken (multiple Play Store apps: com.hyya.prayerpal,
com.ionicerrrrcode.prayerpal). **SalahMate** is free — no app collisions found.
Package: `com.salahmate.app`.

## Identity

**Night sky — devotion.** Deep midnight indigo with soft gold accents.
Crescent-moon calm. Reverent, quiet, beautiful. Nothing like Palate's
saffron, DrySpell's dawn, LanceLedger's pine, or DoseDone's teal.

## Voice

Respectful, warm, encouraging. Short sentences. Never preachy — the app is a
companion, not a judge. Prayer names shown in Arabic + transliteration.

## Concept (the wedge)

Prayer times are the five anchors of a Muslim's day. SalahMate turns them
into a habit system: attach any habit to "after Fajr" / "after Maghrib" and
build streaks on top of the prayers you're already doing. Prayer tracking +
habit stacking + dhikr counter in one calm place.

## Differentiation vs Muslim Pro / Athan apps

1. **Habits anchored to prayers** — habit stacking on the 5 daily anchors
   ("after Fajr: read 2 pages"). Pure prayer-time apps don't do habits; habit
   apps don't know prayer times.
2. **Unified day score** — prayers + habits in one streak system.
3. **Dhikr counter built in** (33/100/1000 targets).
4. **Offline prayer times** (on-device calculation, no network), no account,
   local-first, AR/EN RTL bilingual.

## Honesty notes

- Times are calculated (Muslim World League default); the app says "verify
  with your local mosque" in Settings.
- Qibla compass is NOT in v1 (sensor risk) — documented in BUILD_NOTES.
