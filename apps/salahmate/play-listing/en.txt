Your day, anchored in prayer.

SalahMate gives you accurate prayer times — and turns the five daily
prayers into anchors for the habits you want to build.

ACCURATE PRAYER TIMES, OFFLINE
Prayer times calculated on your device for your location — no internet
needed. Six calculation methods (Muslim World League, Egyptian, Karachi,
ISNA, Umm al-Qura, Tehran). Gentle reminders before each prayer.

TRACK YOUR PRAYERS
One tap marks each prayer done. Watch your week fill up and build a
streak of perfect days.

HABITS ANCHORED TO PRAYER
The companion part: attach any habit to "after Fajr" or "after Maghrib" —
read, exercise, dhikr, anything. Habits ride on prayers you're already
doing, with their own streaks.

ONE DAY SCORE
Prayers + habits combine into a single daily score. Simple, motivating,
never shaming.

DHIKR COUNTER
A beautiful tap counter with 33 / 100 / 500 / 1000 targets and a gentle
vibration when you reach your goal.
