# Privacy Policy — SalahMate

**Effective date:** [launch date] · **Contact:** [support email]

SalahMate ("the app") is a prayer-time and habit companion. This policy
explains what data we handle and why.

## Data stored on your device

Your records — prayers marked, habits, dhikr counts, location used for
prayer times — are stored locally on your device using the app's private
storage. They never leave your device unless you enable cloud backup (see
below).

## Optional cloud backup

If cloud backup is enabled, an encrypted-in-transit copy of your prayer
and habit records is stored on our backend (Supabase) keyed by a random
device identifier. No name, email, or account is required or collected.
You can delete the backup by erasing the app's data or contacting us.

## Location

Your location is used only on-device to calculate prayer times. It is
never transmitted anywhere.

## Notifications

Prayer reminders are scheduled locally on your device. Reminder content
never leaves your device.

## Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Google Play. We receive purchase tokens to
verify your subscription status; we do not see or store your payment
details. Google's privacy policy applies to the payment itself.

## What we do NOT collect

- No advertising identifiers, no ad SDKs, no behavioral tracking.
- No contacts, photos, or files.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

## Changes

We will update this policy with any material change and note the new
effective date above.
