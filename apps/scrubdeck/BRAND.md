# ScrubDeck — Brand

Nursing/medical-exam flashcards with spaced repetition. App #16 of the
portfolio (queue order; claimed as `claimed/16-scrubdeck`).

**Name note:** "ScrubDeck" is clear — no app collisions (only a physical
"Davis's Med Deck" book product shares a similar name). Package:
`com.scrubdeck.app`.

## Identity

**Scrub wine + gold.** Deep wine (the scrubs), warm gold (mastery),
paper white. Clinical but human — confident, not sterile. Nothing like
the other apps' palettes.

## Voice

Senior-nurse-tutor energy: direct, high-yield, encouraging. "Know this
cold for the exam."

## Concept

NCLEX-style flashcards with real SM-2 spaced repetition, pre-loaded —
Anki without the homework of building your own deck:

1. **Study** — daily due cards, flip, grade Again/Hard/Good/Easy.
2. **SM-2** — the algorithm schedules each card's next review.
3. **Decks** — Fundamentals (free), Pharmacology, Med-Surg,
   Maternal/Peds (Plus), plus your own custom decks.
4. **High-yield tags** — pharm suffix decoder cards (-olol, -pril…).

## Differentiation vs Anki / generic flashcard apps

1. **Pre-loaded NCLEX-style nursing decks** — Anki ships empty; students
   burn hours building decks before studying.
2. **Nursing-specific wedges** — drug-suffix decoder cards, lab-value
   ranges, "priority question" framing baked into content.
3. **Dead-simple UX** — 4 grading buttons, no sync servers, no plugins;
   full AR/EN RTL (Anki's Arabic support is poor).
4. **Streaks + mastery rings** per deck — gamified without gimmicks.

## Honesty notes

- Content is study aid, not medical advice — disclaimer in listing.
- SM-2 parameters are standard; "retention" stat is self-grade based.
