# BRAND — ShadowSay (App #15)

## Name decision (collision-checked 2026-10-02)
- **ShadowTalk** (proposed) — REJECTED. Direct in-domain collision: the GitHub
  repo `dadaskis/shadowtalk` is literally an Android shadowing-practice app,
  and "ShadowTalk" is also a published academic prosody-training app (Mrozek 2020).
- **EchoLingo** (fallback 1) — REJECTED. Multiple Android language apps ship
  under this name (Madankk-06 EchoLingo Realtime Translator APK; priyanshuranjan818
  EchoLingo Kotlin app).
- **ParrotTalk** (fallback 2) — REJECTED. In-domain iOS collision: "ParrotTalk"
  AI language flashcards app (Education, Noam Levi) plus "Parrot: Speech
  Improvement" and APKPure backup apps under the same name.
- **Repeatly** (fallback 3) — REJECTED. Live Play Store app "Repeatly – Interval
  Alarm" (com.nmd.repeatly) plus an iOS task manager of the same name.
- **ShadowSay** — CHOSEN. Web + Play search 2026-10-02: no app, no store
  listing, no language-domain product under this name. A "ShadowSay" poem on
  poetry.com and a stale GitHub org named shadowsay (fastjson fork, dead since
  ~2017) are the only hits — no conflict.

## Identity
- App name: **ShadowSay**
- Package: `com.shadowsay.app`
- Tagline: "Shadow. Speak. Sound native." / "ظِلّ، تكَلَّم، وكُن كالناطقين"
- Category: Education

## Palette — designed from scratch for language learning
Speech energy on a calm learning base; warm neutrals so nothing feels cold.
- **Warm coral** (`#D05136` light / `#EA7F66` dark) — the "voice" accent:
  play, record, score, streak. Energy without Duolingo-green sameness.
- **Deep teal** (`#0B1E1F` dark background) — calm, studious, pairs with coral.
- **Cream / warm paper** (`#FFF9F0` light background) — phrase cards read like
  language flashcards; warm grays throughout (cool grays would clash with coral).
- Semantic hues tuned against coral: success `#4C9A6C`, warning `#D9A441`,
  danger `#D6534F`, info `#5B8DD9`.
- Not reused from any sibling: Fable indigo/lavender, Palate saffron,
  KeepsFresh greens/cream, MeetBrief slate/signal-blue.

## Voice
Direct, coach-like, honest about limits ("Honest reps beat fake AI scores").
AR copy is Modern Standard with natural phrasing, not transliterated English.
