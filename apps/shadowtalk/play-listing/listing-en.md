## Full description
Duolingo drills words. ELSA scores you with a black box. ShadowSay trains
the thing that actually makes you sound native: rhythm.

Shadowing is the method polyglots really use — listen to a native phrase,
repeat it out loud in the same rhythm, compare, repeat. ShadowSay turns it
into a daily 5-minute habit:

• NATIVE MODEL VOICE — 40 real-world phrases (travel, work, daily life)
  spoken by on-device TTS in English or Arabic. No internet needed.
• RECORD YOUR ATTEMPT — one tap, then hear yourself back.
• SIDE-BY-SIDE COMPARE — play the model and your attempt one tap apart
  with playback bars, then score yourself 1–5 stars. No fake "AI scores".
• AR ↔ EN BOTH WAYS — packs for Arabic speakers learning English AND
  English speakers learning Arabic.
• STREAKS & PROGRESS — daily streak, per-pack ladders, stars earned.

Free: the Travel pack (or Arabic pack, depending on your direction),
10 practice attempts a day.
ShadowSay Pro: all 4 packs, unlimited practice, free trial.

Your recordings never leave your phone. No account needed.
