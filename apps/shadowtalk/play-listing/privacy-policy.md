# Privacy Policy — ShadowSay

Last updated: 2026-10-02

ShadowSay ("the app") is a language pronunciation practice app. This policy
explains what the app does with your information.

## What the app stores on your device
- Your practice history: self-scored stars, phrases completed, streaks.
- Your voice recordings: saved only in the app's private storage on your
  device. **Recordings are never uploaded, transmitted, or shared.** Deleting
  the app deletes them.

## What the app does NOT do
- No account, no sign-in, no email collection.
- No advertising SDKs, no analytics SDKs, no trackers.
- No automated pronunciation scoring — scores are self-assigned by you.
- The model voice uses your device's built-in text-to-speech (expo-speech);
  phrases are generated on-device and no text is sent to any server.

## Subscriptions
Purchases and free trials are processed by Google Play Billing. We do not
see or store your payment details; Google's privacy policy applies to the
purchase flow.

## Permissions
- Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO): used only to record your practice attempts,
  only when you tap "Record". You can deny it; the model-voice listening
  parts of the app still work.

## Contact
For privacy questions, contact the developer through the Play Store listing.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on the Play
Store listing.
