# TendHome — Brand

Home maintenance reminder log. App #20 of the portfolio (queue order;
claimed as `claimed/20-homecare` — claim is by number per protocol).

**Name note:** "Hearth" (multiple home-maintenance projects), "Mend"
(same-category iOS app), "DwellWell" (exact-concept app with Home Health
Score) all taken. **TendHome** is clear. Package: `com.tendhome.app`.

## Identity

**Terracotta + warm charcoal on cream.** Earthy, trustworthy, lived-in —
tend your home like a garden. Nothing like the other apps' palettes.

## Voice

Helpful neighbor who's handy. Plain, warm, practical. "Small tasks now
beat big repairs later."

## Concept

The underserved niche: homeowners forget the $15 tasks that become $1,500
repairs. TendHome fixes that:

1. **Pre-loaded task library** — 24 recurring home tasks with sane
   intervals (HVAC filter 90d, smoke detectors 30d, gutters 180d…).
2. **"Needs attention" queue** — home screen sorted by urgency: overdue →
   due soon. New tasks show as due now until first done.
3. **Mark done → next due auto-computed**; cost logged per completion.
4. **Local reminders** — notification on the due date, 9 AM.
5. **Cost log** — what you've spent keeping the house healthy (Plus:
   annual insights).
6. **Custom tasks** — add your own with any interval.

## Differentiation vs Todoist / Centriq / HomeZada

1. **Pre-loaded maintenance library** with real intervals — not a blank
   todo list.
2. **Seasonal tags** — spring/fall tasks surface at the right time.
3. **Cost tracking** per task → annual maintenance spend (Plus insights).
4. **AR/EN RTL**, offline-first, no account.

## Honesty notes

- Intervals are sensible defaults, not professional advice — seasonal
  timing varies by climate; the app says so.
- Notification delivery unverified on hardware (same caveat as siblings).
